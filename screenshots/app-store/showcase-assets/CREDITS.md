# Showcase photos

Photos used by the App Store showcase harness (`moro/App/AppStoreScreenshotHarnessView.swift`)
for avatars, the profile cover and post media. All are from Unsplash under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, modification
allowed, attribution not required (credited here anyway). The license does not cover
compiling Unsplash photos into a similar or competing service. People appear only from
behind or at a distance.

The harness reads this folder straight from the checkout when running in the simulator,
so the photos never ship inside the app. Files were resized; the three `post-*` photos
named below as cropped were cut to 3:2, and two avatars were cropped around the subject.
